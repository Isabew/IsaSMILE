About Isabel

Isabel is a 2nd Generation member of KLP48, known for her warm smile and bright personality. A talented pipa player and passionate cosplayer, she enjoys food, cooking, music, and bringing smiles to everyone around her.

  • Birthday: 11 Sept 2008
  • Favorite Color: white and light blue
  • Center: Kerana Ku Suka

About IsaSMILE

IsaSMILE started as a small group of fans who wanted one place to share updates about Isabel without digging through a dozen timelines. Today we run this site, organize the birthday project, and cheer her on together.

Want to help out? Reach out through the credit page — we're always glad to welcome new hands.
